[ Architecture · 003 ]

Four composable modules.
One confidential stack.

Module I
Sealed-Ballot Voting
Threshold-encrypted ballots, publicly verifiable tally (DLEQ proofs), ZK eligibility. Receipt-free by construction.
Module II
Hidden-Until-Execution Proposals
Encrypted payloads with public rules. Reveal on-pass or via timelock — no front-running window.
Module III
Confidential Treasury
Token-2022 Confidential Balances. Auditor keys for selective disclosure. Solvency proofs without exposure.
Module IV
Private Delegation + Eligibility
Prove voting weight without revealing balance. Nullifiers prevent double-voting; identities stay private.
[ How it works · 004 ]

From eligibility to execution.
Six confidential steps.

01
Prove eligibility
ZK proof of voting rights and weight — no balance exposed.
02
Encrypt ballot
Threshold encryption client-side. No plaintext leaves the browser.
03
Cast sealed
Submit as on-chain commitment. Nullifier blocks double-votes.
04
Threshold tally
Only the totals are decrypted, with a 3-of-5 key. On devnet all five shares sit on one server; independent tally nodes are planned.
05
ZK correctness proof
Proves the result is the honest count of exactly the eligible votes.
06
Execute on-chain
Eligibility root and result hash are anchored on Solana; anyone can re-verify the tally.
